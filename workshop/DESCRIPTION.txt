[h1]Divine Fertility[/h1]
Adds the [b]Divine Fertility[/b] trait and two diplomacy interactions to grant or remove it. A character with the trait is blessed with limitless fertility and a spouse who adores them, and passes the blessing on to every child.

[h2]The trait[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Fertility[/b] +9999 and [b]years of fertility[/b] +9999, so age no longer ends fertility
[*] [b]Spouse opinion[/b] +9999
[*] [b]Health[/b] +1
[*] Opposite of [b]Infertile[/b] and [b]Fecund[/b]
[*] [b]Always inherited[/b]: children of a parent with the trait always get it (100%)
[*] Marked as a physical, good trait; available in the ruler designer for [b]99 points[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Interactions[/h2]
In the [b]Diplomacy[/b] category of the character interaction menu:
[list]
[*] [b]Grant Divine Fertility[/b]: gives the trait to any character, instantly
[*] [b]Remove Divine Fertility[/b]: takes it away again, instantly
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Updated for Crusader Kings III [b]1.20 Crozier[/b]
[*] Adds new files only and overrides nothing from the base game
[*] Translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Polish, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Japanese
[/list]
